What it does differently

Offline by design. Everything runs on one machine with no network access — a requirement of the problem statement, not a preference.
Analyses everything, reports the share that matters. Every transaction is examined. The flagged groups are shown as a proportion of the whole.
Explainable by construction. Each score decomposes into the factors behind it, and the parts sum exactly to the number on screen.

What makes the solution different
1
Monitoring, not just a one-time score Stable wallet identities preserve history across batches, so the system can show escalation, dormancy, new entities, and cluster merges.
2
On-chain plus network intelligence Bitcoin flows are joined with IP, country, and network metadata to reveal who moved money and where activity was controlled from.
3
Explainable by construction Every lead shows the factors behind its priority score, with contributions that reconcile to the displayed result instead of hiding the reasoning.
4
Built for sensitive environments The complete system runs locally and offline, with no cloud dependency, no external requests, and no third-party GeoIP lookup.
5
Evidence before action Data-quality gates, drift warnings, estimated fund trails, reports, and analyst acknowledgement keep the human reviewer in control.

Our claim is focused: NETRA is a prioritisation and investigation tool, not an automatic verdict. Its advantage is the complete, auditable path from traffic to lead.
