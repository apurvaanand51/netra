What is in this data?

Analysis of everything the tool read — not only the part it flagged. The suspicious groups are reported as a share of this whole, because "this group moved 62 BTC" means nothing until you know what an ordinary transaction in the same capture looks like.

Where the value actually sits

Bitcoin values span nine orders of magnitude, so an average describes nobody. These are the sizes of individual transactions, by how much they moved.

Where the traffic comes from

Countries across all wallet groups. The flagged count inside each bar is a highlight, not the picture — most traffic is ordinary.

How the wallets behave

Every group counted exactly once, under the first behaviour it matches. A group can show several patterns, so counting them separately would sum past 100% and mislead.
